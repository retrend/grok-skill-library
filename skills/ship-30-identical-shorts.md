# Steps

1. Define the product shape once and keep it fixed (from source):
   - 65–90 words of script
   - One AI voice, picked once and never changed
   - Stock footage or screen capture
   - Word-by-word captions centered on screen
   - Quiet bed track from the free YouTube Audio Library
   - 25–35 seconds runtime
   - ~20 minutes to make once the template file exists
   - No camera, face, studio, or advanced editing — drag clips into the template

2. Ignore vanity “bar” work that does not move numbers (unique idea theater, personality, 4K fetish, perfect edit, logo, hashtag/time superstition) until the format has a measured floor.

3. Commit to **30 videos in one identical shape**, changing only the topic. Treat early view counts as noise until ~30; ~9 videos is not enough signal. Do not switch formats after a weak video — that restarts the clock.

4. Optimize the five levers **in this order**:
   1. **Who watches** — niche decides CPM before upload one (source cites Americans 25–45 ~$0.25–$0.40 per 1k views vs teens ~$0.05; treat figures as directional from the post, not guaranteed rates).
   2. **The idea** — one flat sentence you’d say at a bus stop. Concrete stakes beat listicle bait that dies by 0:06.
   3. **First frame** — must look different from other first frames in the niche or the thumb scrolls before the writing matters.
   4. **Change every 4–6 seconds** — new information, raised stake, or contradiction. Transition effects do not count.
   5. **Cut the last ~8 seconds** — payoff, then stop. No outro; post-payoff seconds hurt completion rate (what YouTube pays on, per source).

5. Day-one action (do before closing the loop):
   - Open YouTube on a fresh account
   - Search the niche you’ve been sitting on
   - Watch 20 Shorts all the way through; like the good ones
   - Next day the feed surfaces niche outliers — that is the start of research

## Success check

- Template locked; voice/caption/length unchanged across videos
- Topic is the only variable across a run toward 30
- Each short ends on payoff with no outro padding

## Anti-patterns

- Bookmarking guides instead of doing the day-one watch pass
- Changing format after ~9 posts and calling it “iteration”
- Optimizing logos, hashtags, or posting times before niche + idea + first frame + pacing + ending
- Not a general growth-hack or “get rich” skill — only this Shorts production floor test

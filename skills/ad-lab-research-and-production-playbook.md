# Ad Lab research and production playbook

Load this when Steven wants research-led creative, not as a substitute for the live profile's approval and spend rules.

## Competitor ads and customer language

Research relevant competitor ads through Meta Ad Library and TikTok Creative Center where available. When no period is given, use the last 30 days and state the exact date range and retrieval date. Record observed start dates and sort by observed longevity when useful, noting gaps in library coverage.

Collect customer objections and desires from ad comments, relevant reviews and discussions, including Amazon, Trustpilot and Reddit where relevant. Group these findings with exact customer quotes and source links. Never invent or silently paraphrase a quotation. Keep source links and dates. Separate observed creative patterns from performance evidence. An ad's longevity, views or presence in an ad library does not establish profitability or conversions. Do not call a competitor ad a winner without performance data.

## Angles and production briefs

Group the research into patterns before writing angles. Turn those patterns into distinct angles grounded in the offer and audience. Explain the hook, audience objection or desire, proof, offer and call to action, with a short rationale and supporting ad and customer-source links. Ask Steven to choose angles before writing production briefs, unless he explicitly requests finished briefs or scripts.

For chosen angles or an explicit production request, write scene-by-scene briefs with spoken lines, pacing, on-screen text, b-roll reference links, CTA and delivery specs. Keep UGC scripts within the requested duration and include what to show and shot suggestions. Link sourced decisions to the ad, transcript line, timestamp or comment behind them. Label original creative choices and hypotheses as such rather than inventing supporting evidence or cutting useful original ideas merely because they lack a source.

## Video breakdowns

Analyse actual video with the video-analysis capability, not a transcript alone. Give timestamped breakdowns of the first three seconds, including the first frame, spoken hook and on-screen text, then cuts and scene changes, proof moments and CTA. Transcribe spoken lines when needed. State the model's sampling limits and OCR uncertainty. Fast cuts or brief text may be missed, so do not claim frame-by-frame completeness without that coverage. Check key opening frames, proof and text where possible before using them in briefs, and flag anything needing human review. Coordinate production with Cut Room or Cove Edit Studio when Steven requests it. Preserve original work and rights; do not copy competitors' scripts or invent testimonials.

## Audits and experiments

Audit where spend went, who saw the ads, what changed, creative fatigue and business outcomes. Compare equal-length periods and account for missing days, seasonality, tracking changes and conversion lag. Separate account observations, hypotheses and source claims in plain prose. Each experiment states its hypothesis, variable, primary metric, sample or spend rationale, duration and stop rule before launch. Report inconclusive results honestly; do not scale from noisy early returns.

## Ambient-specific rules

1. Name the listening purpose for each single (focus, wind-down, walking, background) and put it in the title or the first description line. A 3 to 5 minute single does a different job from an hour-long mix, so don't package it like one.
2. Titles: track name first, then a plain mood or setting cue. Example: "Field Margins · ambient techno with summer meadow footage". No hype words.
3. Description: line 1 is the mood and purpose; then the track family, the tempo or feel if known, and the AI-music disclosure wording Steven approved. Add playlist and next-single links once they exist. Add no footage credits or sources.
4. Use one playlist per track family plus a full-releases playlist in release order. End screens point to the next single or the family playlist.
5. A later compilation mix made only from released singles is an option. Flag the reused-content risk and let Steven decide.
6. Vary the footage and colour treatment between tracks. A strict identical template across 26 AI-music uploads is the pattern the inauthentic-content policy targets, so don't borrow ship-30-identical-shorts' fixed template for the long-form singles.

## Never borrow

- From run-a-multi-channel-shorts-network: burner channels, rival pairs, comment seeding from second accounts, fake first comments. Only its account-hygiene step applies (roles for editors, 2FA, current recovery email).
- Engagement bait, sub4sub, fake giveaways, or misleading thumbnails.
- Monetisation timelines, CTR scores or revenue numbers that are not from Studio or official pages.

## Measurement once live

- Compare each single at 7 and 28 days against the others at the same ages.
- Long-form: impressions click-through rate (CTR), average percentage viewed, returning viewers, and playlist adds. Shorts: stayed-to-watch rate and the click-through to the linked single.
- CTR and impressions come from a Studio Advanced-mode CSV export. Willow Phase has no Markifact or Metricool connection.
- Wait for at least 6 to 8 singles before changing the format. One weak upload is noise.

# Steps

Pick ONE path first. Path 1 (audience + funnel) underpins the others.

## Path 1: Grow an audience and funnel it to your offer
For people with an existing product or service.
1. **Map the funnel.** Aim for roughly 60% top-of-funnel posts (reach), 30% middle (trust), 10% bottom (sales).
> Using my brief, pillars and recent posts, map my content to a funnel. Tell me what % of my posts are TOF, MOF and BOF, what's missing, and give me a weekly posting mix that grows my audience while driving people to [PRODUCT_OR_SERVICE].
2. **Create content that sells.** Core MOF/BOF pillars: case studies, results (screenshots, numbers, before/after), behind the scenes, frameworks, lessons.
> Using my results, client wins and experience, give me 10 MOF/BOF post ideas that prove I can deliver [OUTCOME]. Focus on case studies, results and behind the scenes. Match my voice doc.
3. **Turn the profile into a sales page.**
> Audit my X profile like a conversion expert. Review my bio, pinned post, banner and link. Tell me what's unclear, what's missing, and rewrite each one to drive people to [PRODUCT_OR_SERVICE].
4. **Add soft CTAs** to your best posts, e.g. "I break this down in my newsletter (link in bio)" or "DM me 'system' and I'll send the template."
> For any post I send you, suggest 3 soft CTAs that point to [PRODUCT_OR_SERVICE] without sounding salesy. Match my voice doc.
5. **Track what converts** weekly.
> Each week, report which posts drove the most profile visits, link clicks, DMs and new followers. Flag the topics and formats that convert best so I can make more of them.

## Path 2: Lead generation (fastest)
1. Define a qualified lead (attach emails or prospect briefs if you have them).
> Using my brief and services, define my ideal client: industry, account size, stage, budget signals, and the specific problems I solve for them. Then list the signals on X that show someone is a good lead right now.
2. Build a "Lead Scout" bot.
> You're my Lead Scout. Every day, search X for people who match my ideal client and show buying signals: hiring posts, people asking for help with [SERVICE], founders who just raised or launched, and accounts with strong products but weak content. Find 10 leads, explain why each one is a fit, and score them 1 to 10. Send the list to [DESTINATION] by [TIME].
3. Draft outreach: write the template outline yourself (to avoid AI slop), let the bot fill in each message, and **you approve and send manually**.
4. Tips: scrape for people posting about the pain you solve, run outreach on more than one channel, have the bot build a pipeline tracker.

## Path 4: Sell bot setup as a service
1. Build useful systems for yourself first (content, research, coding), then document them publicly on X.
2. Turn your setup into a template.
> Document my entire bot setup as a reusable template: my coordinator instructions, every bot's role, prompt and routine, and how they work together. Remove anything personal so I can adapt it for a client.
3. Package the offer: name, who it's for, what's in setup, training and retainer, pricing. Put it on a site, ideally with a video sales letter.
4. Find clients with the Lead Scout from Path 2, tuned to this offer:
> Find 10 people on X in [NICHE] who are posting about AI agents, asking how to use bots, or clearly struggling to keep up with content. Score each 1 to 10 and explain why they'd benefit from a bot team.
5. Deliver: weekly check-ins, bot tune-ups, skills management, add-ons like a second brain in Obsidian or Notion.
- Service ideas: content teams for founders, X account audits, selling templates and skills, lead-gen agents for agencies, workshops or 1-on-1 setup calls.

## Path 5: Build an offer from zero (existing skills)
1. Skills interview:
> Interview me one question at a time to uncover every skill, result and piece of expertise I have. Ask about my work history, side projects, things people ask me for help with, and problems I've solved for myself. Then list everything I could realistically package into an offer.
2. Market demand:
> Search X for what people in [NICHE] are struggling with, asking for help on, or complaining about. Also scan my replies, DMs and comments from the last 60 days. List the top 10 problems and any existing offers that solve them (with prices if visible). Then compare them to my skills and rank the 3 strongest overlaps.
3. Package it:
> Package my top overlap into an offer. Give me a name, who it's for, the outcome, the format, a launch price, and a one-paragraph pitch for my bio or pinned post.
4. **Validate, then sell.** Test demand with posts, polls, long-form, reply strategy or a free/discounted offer.
> Write 5 posts to validate [OFFER] this week: a problem post, a poll, a behind-the-scenes post, a waitlist post and a soft pre-sell. Match my voice doc.
5. Once validated, build bots for client acquisition (organic content, outreach).

## Path 6: Get hired
> You're my Job Application Agent. I want to apply for [ROLE] at [COMPANY]. Job post: [LINK].
> 1. Research the company (site, X account, news): what they do, customers, brand voice, launches or funding, what content works. Identify the 3 biggest problems this role is hired to solve.
> 2. Break down the job post (skills, keywords, outcomes). Compare to my brief and results. Tell me where I'm strong, weak, and how to position gaps honestly.
> 3. Rewrite my CV for this role, one page, using job-post keywords naturally. Only real experience and results from my brief. Never invent or exaggerate. Ask me if something is missing.
> 4. Draft a cover letter under 200 words on how I'd help in the first 90 days, plus one piece of proof (a quick content audit, 5 post ideas, or a 30-day plan).
> 5. Open the application page and fill the form. Upload the CV as a PDF. STOP before submitting and send me a screenshot and summary. Submit only when I say "approved".
> 6. Log company, role, date, link and status. Remind me to follow up in 5 days and draft a short follow-up.

## Guardrails (added, not in source)
- Review before anything goes out: no auto-DMs, auto-replies or auto-applications. Follow X's automation and spam rules.
- Don't invent results, testimonials or credentials in sales content or CVs.
- Disclose paid sponsorships as the platform requires.
- Income figures are anecdotes, not promises.

# Not for
- Generic "get rich" content with no existing skill, offer or audience to build on.
- Growing the audience itself. Path 1 assumes you've got a content system already.
- Automating mass outreach or fake engagement.

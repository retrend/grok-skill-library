# Steps

## Before launch
1. **Write your target on a post-it.** Every rule below is measured against it.
   ```
   sales
   target per sale = profit on an average order ÷ 3
   e.g. $75 profit per order → $25 a sale

   leads
   max per lead = what a customer is worth × how many leads become customers
   e.g. $600 customer, 1 in 10 leads buys → $60 a lead
   target = $40, so you keep a margin
   ```
   A lead is an enquiry, not a customer.
2. **Set up seven columns.** Amount spent; results (purchases or leads only); cost per result against target; frequency (per person this week); link click-through rate (link clicks, not all clicks); hook rate; hold rate. Build the last two once under Columns, Customize columns, Create custom metric, and save as a preset:
   ```
   hook rate = 3-second video plays ÷ impressions
   hold rate = ThruPlays ÷ 3-second video plays
   ```
   Rough bar: hook under 20% is weak, 30%+ good; hold 25%+ good. Your own account's average beats any benchmark. Meta reports the same sale several ways (purchases, website purchases, pixel purchases): pick one, never add them up.
3. **Brake and settings.** Set Billing & payments, Account spending limit so what's left covers this month's budget plus ~10%; raise it monthly. After every launch, open the ad's Advantage+ creative settings and switch off everything that changes your words or picture (rewrites, music, animation, product cards, translation). Keep "adapt to placement". Recheck: options can switch back on.

## Daily verdicts
4. **Don't judge too early.** No verdict on an ad before 1,000 impressions and 4 days live. Then judge on the last 14 days together, never yesterday. Learning wants ~50 results a week per ad set; "learning limited" is normal on small accounts. Sales are credited back to the ad's day for up to 7 days after a click, so recent days keep growing.
5. **Check the whole account first.**
   - Results crashed everywhere but clicks held: tracking problem. Touch nothing.
   - Everything ~25% more expensive at once (season, Black Friday): move the bar with it.
   - Lots of clicks, few people reaching the page: the site is the problem.
6. **Give every ad one verdict.**
   - **Wait:** under 1,000 impressions or under 4 days.
   - **Kill:** 0 results after spending 3× target over two weeks; or over 1.5× target per result over two weeks and still expensive this week; or a video nobody watches past 3 seconds that doesn't pay back.
   - **Refresh:** idea works, people are tired: frequency 3+ a week with cost rising, or link CTR down 25% while cost is up 20%. Warm audiences (past visitors, customers) can take up to 6 a week.
   - **Scale:** under target over two weeks with at least 5 sales or leads.
   - **Keep:** everything else.
   - **Watch:** one bad week after a good one. Don't kill.
7. **Scale slowly.** +20% at a time, then hands off for 3 days. Scale the budget, not the ad: raise a campaign or ad set budget only when the whole thing is under target; if one ad is a winner inside an expensive campaign, switch off the weak ads first. Before raising, all four must hold: whole budget under target over two weeks; spending 90%+ of budget; not in learning; nothing changed in 3 days. If one fails, that's today's answer.
8. **Make 2–3 moves max, then log.** One log entry per day: what you saw, what you did, why. When unsure: wait.

## Next ads
9. **Change 2 of 4.** Meta's delivery system (Andromeda) groups lookalike ads as one ad, so recolours find no new buyers. A new ad changes at least two of: the idea (reason to buy), the person, the format, the opening (first 3 seconds or first line). Run 3 to 6 live ads per ad set, each a different idea; more is wasted under about $50 a day. A tired winner keeps its idea and gets a new opening (new first frame or scene, not just new words).
10. **Start from your winners.** High hook rate (30%+): the opening works. High link CTR: the promise works. Low cost per sale: page and offer work too. Fill this brief for each new ad:
    ```
    name: A7 · <IDEA_IN_3_WORDS>
    kind: <ITERATION_OF_A1> or <NEW_IDEA>
    idea: <THE_REASON_TO_BUY_ONE_LINE>
    for: <THE_PERSON>
    format: <VIDEO_9x16_15-25s> <PHOTO_4x5> <CAROUSEL_3-5_CARDS>
    opening: <EXACTLY_WHAT_IS_SEEN_AND_SAID_IN_FIRST_3S_OR_FIRST_LINE>
    then: <2-4_BEATS>
    proof: <A_REAL_NUMBER_REVIEW_OR_VISIBLE_DETAIL>
    ask: <THE_BUTTON_PLUS_ONE_PLAIN_INSTRUCTION>
    why it should work: <THE_WINNER_NUMBER_IT_BUILDS_ON>
    ```
    Proof has to be real. If you don't have one, leave it empty and go get one.
11. **Write the first 125 characters as the ad.** Line 1 is the hook: a specific outcome, a surprising fact or a customer's own words, never "introducing…" or a question nobody asked. Then 1–3 short lines (proof, what it is, offer) and one plain instruction. Headline about 40 characters, the benefit, not the brand name. Video: 9:16 for reels and stories, 4:5 for feed, captions always, keep text out of the top ~14% and bottom ~35% of a reel. Openings that work: result first then how; a hand doing the thing close up with sound; a customer saying one specific sentence; on-screen text naming the person; something visibly wrong getting fixed. A captioned phone video often beats a polished ad. Don't reference the viewer's personal attributes ("struggling with debt?"); talk about the product.

## Competitors
12. **Read the Meta Ad Library by start date.** Link (change country and keyword):
    ```
    https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=<COUNTRY>&q=<BRAND_OR_KEYWORD>&search_type=keyword_unordered&media_type=all
    ```
    Running 60+ days: proven, study these. Under 14 days: their tests. Many near-identical ads launched the same day: a test; come back in 3 weeks to see what survived. For 3–5 competitors, 5–10 ads each, write:
    ```
    <COMPETITOR> · <N> active · <N> proven (60+ days)
    proven ideas: <THE_REASON_TO_BUY_EACH_PROVEN_AD_SELLS>
    openings that last: <FIRST_LINES_OR_SECONDS_THAT_KEEP_COMING_BACK>
    offers: <DISCOUNTS_SHIPPING_GUARANTEES_BUNDLES>
    what nobody says: <THE_ANGLE_MISSING_ACROSS_ALL_OF_THEM>
    ```
    The last line is usually your best next ad. Take the idea, never copy the ad.

## Lead ads
13. **Judge leads on quality, not cost.** Reply within the first hour; decide before launch who replies and on which channel. Mark every lead: replied, qualified (call booked, quote asked), sale, no reply, fake. Never scale an ad until at least 1 in 10 of its leads is real; never kill one that brings real prospects because its leads look expensive. Many bad leads: switch the form to higher intent (review screen before submit) and add one multiple-choice qualifying question (budget, timing, need), max 3 questions beyond name, email and phone. Few good leads: leave the form, look at the ad.

## Rules Meta enforces
14. **Check categories and tracking.**
    - Special ad categories (housing, employment, credit and financial, politics) are law. Housing can include home repairs and renovations. In the first three: no age or gender targeting, no zip codes, 15-mile (25 km) minimum radius, no lookalikes.
    - EU ads must state who benefits and who pays; Meta no longer runs political or social issue ads in the EU.
    - Health and wellness sites may be limited in what the pixel sends and may not be able to optimise for purchases; check Events Manager first.
    - Interest exclusions are gone; excluding custom audiences (past buyers) still works. With Advantage+ audience on, interests and ages above your minimum are hints. Broad targeting plus genuinely different ads beats clever targeting.
    - Default attribution: 7-day click, 1-day view, plus 1-day engage-through. Meta narrowed what counts as a click this year; check this before blaming ads for a 2026 drop.
    - Meta won't match your shop. Use shop, CRM and bank for money; use Meta's numbers to compare ads with each other.

## As an agent (optional)
15. **Run it as a morning check.** Each morning the agent reads the last 14 days by day at ad level, prints a verdict per ad with the number behind it, proposes 2–3 moves, writes the next ad brief, lists leads still waiting for a call, checks Advantage+ creative toggles, and moves nothing without approval. Every approved move goes in the log.

# Success check
- A written target per sale or lead.
- Every live ad has a verdict and the number behind it, from the last 14 days.
- No more than 2–3 moves a day, each logged with the reason.
- After a month, the log shows which ideas and openings win in your account.

# Anti-patterns
- Switching an ad off on day 2 because it has $11 spent and 0 sales.
- Doubling the budget of a day-1 winner.
- Judging on yesterday instead of two weeks.
- Adding purchases, website purchases and pixel purchases together.
- Ten colour variants of one ad counted as a test.
- Scaling one ad's ad set when the campaign as a whole is over target.
- Cheapest cost per lead treated as best.
- Leaving Advantage+ creative rewriting your ads unchecked.
- Made-up reviews or numbers in the brief.
- Letting an agent change budgets or switch ads off without your approval.
- Running financial-adviser or credit ads without the special ad category and a compliance review. (added, not in source)

# Ship 9 information-gain assets in under 24 hours

Add **information gain** to pages and posts so AI Search can cite unique, specific, quantitative facts competitors lack. Ship any of these nine asset types in under a day.

## Goal

Rank for customer-intent keywords in AI Search by publishing content only you can produce: first-party data, real tests, named reviews, and lived competitor experience.

## Inputs

- `[COMPANY]` / product context
- Access to dashboards, reviews, customers, and (ideally) competitor trial accounts
- Privacy review before publishing any first-party usage data

## The 9 assets (pick what you can ship today)

1. **Dashboard screenshots**  
   Show real numbers (anonymized is fine) nobody else can publish.

2. **Short product YouTube walkthrough**  
   Show how it’s used and answer frequent questions on camera.

3. **Real reviews, quoted + screenshotted**  
   Pull from Google / Trustpilot / Reddit. Prefer reviews with specific likes/dislikes — not “loved the product!”

4. **Before/after from a real project**  
   Include numbers, timelines, and screenshots.  
   Example: “We ran this for 30 days and traffic went from X to Y.”

5. **Named customer/client quote**  
   Include name, title, photo, a real opinion. Bonus if it mildly disagrees with the obvious industry take.

6. **Afternoon test you (or the team) can run**  
   Example: ask ChatGPT “what do you think about `[COMPANY]`” for 50 users; publish what happened, interesting data, and correlation with your top users’ AI sentiment scores.

7. **First-party stat**  
   From SaaS usage or similar. Include sample size and analysis methodology. Never publish anything that violates user privacy.

8. **Competitor comparison from real use**  
   Don’t rebuild “[Competitor] vs [Competitor]” from marketing sites. Sign up, run real flows, capture screenshots, pros/cons, and gotchas.  
   Example: “the free plan caps at 3 users and that’s not on the pricing page.”

9. **Dated experiment timeline**  
   List what you tested, outcomes, and dates.  
   Example: “We migrated from WordPress to Framer on March 21st; here’s traffic over the following 90 days.”

## How to run (same day)

1. Pick 1–3 asset types you can finish in &lt;24 hours.  
2. Gather evidence (screenshots, quotes, numbers, trial notes).  
3. Publish on the money page, comparison page, or a dedicated post that links from those pages.  
4. Prefer crawlable HTML (text + images with context) over image-only claims.  
5. Pair with related moves: methodology/competitor scoring tables, About Us key-facts, GSC striking-distance BOFU pages.

## Success check

- Asset includes specifics only you could produce (numbers, names, dates, screenshots, lived UX)  
- Claims are attributable and privacy-safe  
- Published where AI and buyers evaluating the offer will see it

## Anti-patterns

- Generic “trusted by teams worldwide” fluff  
- Reviews or quotes with no specifics  
- Competitor pages written only from their marketing copy  
- Stats with no sample size / method  
- Publishing identifiable user data

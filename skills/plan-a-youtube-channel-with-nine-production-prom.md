The source's "BREAKING", "100% free" and "hit monetization in just 45 days" claims are unverified marketing. YouTube Partner Programme thresholds change; check YouTube Help. "Free" ignores equipment, software and time.

Single-channel loop. For a faceless multi-channel network use run-a-multi-channel-shorts-network. (added, not in source)

Run in order. Fill every placeholder with real facts; mark unverified claims instead of inventing them.

## Inputs
- <SKILLS>, <EQUIPMENT>, <HOURS_PER_WEEK>
- <NICHE>, <AUDIENCE>, <FORMAT> once chosen
- Viewer comments, search suggestions or competing titles
- <VIDEO_IDEAS>, <TOPIC>, <LENGTH>, demos, test results or notes
- Proof you can actually show in a thumbnail
- Per-video results from YouTube Studio
- <COUNTRY> and current Studio Earn totals or pasted official requirements

## Steps
1. **Choose your niche**
   ```
   Using <SKILLS>, <EQUIPMENT>, and <HOURS_PER_WEEK>, suggest three narrow YouTube niches. For each, name the exact viewer, their recurring problem, and three videos I can film. Rank the niches by filming effort and depth of future topics. Give me two YouTube searches to check demand.
   ```
2. **Set up your channel**
   ```
   My niche is <NICHE>, my viewer is <AUDIENCE>, and my format is <FORMAT>. Write five channel names, a one-line promise, an About section, and banner text under eight words. Propose three recurring series and pick three launch videos that naturally lead into one another.
   ```
3. **Turn viewer questions into videos**
   ```
   Analyze <FIVE_VIEWER_COMMENTS_SEARCH_SUGGESTIONS_OR_COMPETING_TITLES>. Group them by viewer problem and pitch eight original videos. For each, write a working title and suggest a demo, test, or example I can make. Pick the top three and show which input supports each.
   ```
4. **Plan your first 45 days**
   ```
   Turn <VIDEO_IDEAS> into a 45-day schedule for <FORMAT> using <EQUIPMENT>, <HOURS_PER_WEEK>, and a $0 budget. Assign dates and time estimates for filming, editing, publishing, and reviewing results. Keep each week within my time limit. Give me day one's shot list and reserve a follow-up slot.
   ```
   It's a production plan, not a monetisation deadline. (added, not in source)
5. **Script a video people can follow**
   ```
   Write a <LENGTH> script for <TOPIC> using <DEMO_TEST_RESULTS_OR_PERSONAL_NOTES>. Open by showing the result. Pair timestamped narration with visuals, demonstrate one common mistake and its fix, and end with <VIEWERS_NEXT_STEP>. Mark claims I need to verify with <CHECK>.
   ```
   Don't publish any line still marked <CHECK>. (added, not in source)
6. **Pair your title with your thumbnail**
   ```
   For <TOPIC>, my viewer wants <RESULT> and I can show <PROOF>. Write five title-and-thumbnail pairs with distinct angles. Describe each thumbnail using <AVAILABLE_SCREENSHOTS_OR_PHOTOS> and up to four words. Pick the clearest pair and write an opening line that delivers on its promise.
   ```
7. **Catch problems before publishing**
   ```
   Review <SCRIPT_OR_SHOT_LIST>, <TITLE_AND_THUMBNAIL>, and <ASSET_SOURCES_AND_PERMISSIONS>. Flag reused or repetitive material, unclear usage rights, possible AI disclosure needs, and promises the video misses. Give me one practical fix per issue and a five-point pre-upload checklist.
   ```
8. **Let analytics pick your next video**
   ```
   Review <PER_VIDEO_RESULTS: format, days live, views, subscribers gained, and available metrics>. Compare same-format uploads at similar ages. Use click-through rate and early retention for long-form, or stayed-to-watch rate and average percentage viewed for Shorts. Flag thin data, then suggest one follow-up, one change to test, and one success metric.
   ```
9. **Check how close you are to monetisation**
   ```
   I'm in <COUNTRY>. Using <YOUTUBE_STUDIO_EARN_TOTALS_AND_STATUS> and <ELIGIBLE_GAINS_OVER_14_OR_28_DAYS>, verify current requirements with official YouTube Help or <PASTED_REQUIREMENTS>. Separate fan funding from ad revenue. Calculate my remaining gap, estimate day-45 progress at this pace, and tell me whether to apply or what to focus on next.
   ```

## Success check
- Niche chosen, channel promise written, three linked launch videos.
- 45-day schedule fits your hours and gear, with a day-one shot list.
- Each script opens on the result and has no open <CHECK> marks; title/thumbnail pair uses proof you have.
- Pre-publish pass done; next video chosen from real Studio data or flagged as thin data.
- Monetisation checked against official requirements.

## Anti-patterns
- Promising monetisation in 45 days or any fixed window.
- Inventing comments, proof, metrics or thresholds.
- Footage or music you lack rights to.
- Skipping AI disclosure or licensing because the budget is $0.

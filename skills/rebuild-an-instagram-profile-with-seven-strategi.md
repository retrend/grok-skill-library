# Rebuild an Instagram profile with seven strategist prompts

Seven role prompts, run in order in one chat. The first five fix what a visitor sees; prompt 6 keeps new followers coming back; prompt 7 tests the changes.

## Inputs (gather first)
- `<NICHE>` and `<IDEAL_FOLLOWER>`
- `<OFFER>` and where the bio link goes
- Current name field, bio, highlight titles, pinned posts (paste as text)
- Screenshots or captions of your three best posts, best carousel, best reels and best stories
- Your last nine grid posts (screenshot)
- 2–3 competitor profiles
- Top questions followers ask; words they search
- Insights for the last 30 days: profile visits, follows from profile, link taps, top posts

## Steps
1. Open one chat. Paste a context block first (added, not in source; the AI can't see your account unless you paste it):
   ```
   Context for the prompts that follow. My niche: <NICHE>. Ideal follower: <IDEAL_FOLLOWER>. Offer: <OFFER>. Current name field: <NAME_FIELD>. Current bio: <BIO>. Highlights: <HIGHLIGHTS>. Pinned posts: <PINNED>. Best posts, carousel, reels, stories: <BEST_CONTENT>. Competitors: <COMPETITORS>. Follower questions: <QUESTIONS>. Search words: <SEARCH_WORDS>. Last 30 days: <INSIGHTS>. Use only this information; say "unknown" where something is missing; don't invent stats, testimonials or results.
   ```
2. Run prompt 1 (bio first; the author says everything hangs on it):
   ```
   Act as an Instagram growth strategist and brand copywriter. Build a complete profile system for my account. Analyze my niche, my ideal follower, my three best posts and the profiles I compete with. Design my bio in three versions, my name field keywords, my link strategy, my highlight covers and titles, and my pinned post plan. My niche is <NICHE>.
   ```
3. Prompt 2, Highlight Story Map:
   ```
   Act as an Instagram content strategist. Turn my highlights into a guided tour of my account. Analyze my offer, the top questions my followers ask, my best stories and my niche. Design five highlight categories, the story order inside each one, the text on every first slide and a closing slide that points new visitors to my pinned posts.
   ```
4. Prompt 3, Pinned Post Trio:
   ```
   Act as a social media creative director. Plan the three posts I pin at the top of my Instagram grid. Analyze my niche, my audience's biggest problem, my best carousel and my offer. Design one post that explains who I help, one that proves it with a story and one that tells visitors what to do next, with hooks and captions for each.
   ```
5. Prompt 4, Bio Keyword Lab:
   ```
   Act as an Instagram search specialist. Make my profile easy to find when people search my topic. Analyze my niche, the words my audience types, my current name field and my bio. Design five name field options, a keyword list ranked by intent, three bio rewrites that use them naturally and the caption phrases I should repeat across posts.
   ```
6. Prompt 5, Grid Audit:
   ```
   Act as a brand designer and Instagram consultant. Audit my last nine posts the way a first-time visitor sees them. Analyze the colors, the cover text, the formats and the topics. Design a grid style guide with a color palette, a cover template, a format rotation and a list of posts to archive or refresh, in order of impact.
   ```
7. Prompt 6, New Follower Welcome:
   ```
   Act as a community manager for Instagram creators. Build the system that turns a new follower into a regular. Analyze my content pillars, my best reels, my story habits and my offer. Design a welcome story sequence, a reply template for new comments, a weekly question sticker plan and a monthly highlight refresh routine.
   ```
8. Prompt 7, Profile Test Plan:
   ```
   Act as a growth analyst for Instagram. Design a four-week test plan for my profile. Analyze my profile visits, my follows from the profile, my link taps and my top posts. Design the order of changes to test, one change per week, the metrics to log, the decision rule for each test and a simple tracking sheet I can fill in.
   ```
9. Pick one bio version and one name field, then ship the changes in the order prompt 7 gives, one per week. Log visits, follows from profile and link taps every week (added, not in source: write down the baseline before week 1).

## Success check
- Bio, name field, highlights, three pins and grid guide are all written and live.
- Baseline plus four weekly rows in the tracking sheet.
- Each change kept or rolled back by its decision rule, not by gut feel.

## Anti-patterns
- Running the prompts without pasting real profile data; you'll get generic copy.
- Changing bio, pins and grid all in one week, so you can't tell what worked.
- Keyword-stuffing the name field until it no longer reads as a name.
- Letting the AI invent testimonials, client results or follower counts for the proof post (added, not in source).
- In regulated niches (finance, health), publishing bio or pin claims without a compliance check (added, not in source).

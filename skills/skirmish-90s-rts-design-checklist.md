# Skirmish 90s RTS design checklist

Use this as the design checklist. "In the spirit of" Command & Conquer, Total Annihilation and Age of Empires means the systems and feel, never the assets. No factions, units, names, logos, music, sprites or maps from those games or any other game.

## Economy

One clear loop. Pick per game: harvesters on a finite resource (C&C), metal and energy flow with no gathering unit (Total Annihilation), or workers on food, wood, gold and stone with ages (Age of Empires).

## Base building

A build queue, a placement grid, a power or supply limit, a tech tree that unlocks units and buildings.

## Army control

Drag-select, control groups, right-click move and attack, rally points, waypoints, fog of war and a minimap.

## Combat

Simple counters (a rock-paper-scissors triangle), unit stats players can read, one or two special abilities per faction.

## Presentation

Sidebar or bottom-panel UI, chunky pixel sprites, a 640x480 feel scaled up, a voiced announcer, short briefing screens before missions.

## Modes

Skirmish against an AI first, then a short campaign of two or three missions with objectives.

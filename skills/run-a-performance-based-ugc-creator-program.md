# Run a performance-based UGC creator program

Source: Hector Serrano ([@hectorserrranoo](https://x.com/hectorserrranoo/status/2108260769603486100)), marketing hire #2 at Wispr Flow, 8 Oct 2026. It's a 15-minute video: "i built @WisprFlow's paid ads program — here's how i'd rebuild it in 30 days". In practice the video covers the creator program only. It does not cover campaign structure, bidding, targeting, or tracking, so pair this with [bulk-launch-meta-ads-from-a-template-ad](sand-workflow:bulk-launch-meta-ads-from-a-template-ad) and [warehouse-meta-ads-api-for-mutations-only](sand-workflow:warehouse-meta-ads-api-for-mutations-only).

Wispr Flow's benchmark is 500–700 net-new UGC ads launched every month.

## 1. Platform and performance model
- Stop paying thousands upfront for creatives that might not convert. Pay for performance instead.
- The loop: a creator joins your group, makes a video, you launch it as an ad, and you pay them a share of what it drives.
- Hector runs this on a creator marketplace called Trybe, which he describes as "TikTok Shop for every platform" (Meta, TikTok, AppLovin). Any equivalent tool, or a spreadsheet plus ad-ID tracking, works too.

## 2. Payment
- E-commerce brands pay a % of the GMV the ad drives.
- SaaS and info products pay a % of the ad spend behind the creator's videos, because their conversion cycles are long. Wispr pays 7% of ad spend.
- **Smart retainer:** this is a guaranteed monthly minimum for hitting a video quota, so new creators without performance history stay motivated. Wispr's version is 25 videos for $1,000 a month.
  - If commissions fall below the retainer, you top them up. For example, $500 earned plus a $500 top-up.
  - If commissions beat the retainer, you pay nothing extra. For example, they earn $3,000 and you add $0.

## 3. Recruit a few intent-based creators, not hundreds
- Don't hire hundreds of mercenary creators making identical talking-head videos. Meta clusters near-duplicate ads (roughly 60%+ similar) and suppresses their reach.
- Start with 4–5 creators chosen to fill gaps in your ad library: new ICPs (your ideal customer profiles), new formats, and new locations or languages.
- Source them off-platform at first, since you'll have no reviews yet. Use connections and creator groups.
- Instagram keyword search also works. Search terms like "UGC", "creator", "tech", and "SaaS", find one creator who meets your bar, then mine their followers, likes, and commenters for similar peers.
- Send 100–200 DMs over a few days to form the first group.

## 4. Culture: treat creators like an in-house sales team
- Transactional, box-checking relationships lead to churn and to your best creators getting poached.
- Offer 1:1 strategy calls. Wispr uses Calendly links to Hector.
- Run a weekly group call. Wispr's is on Thursdays with the marketer and the media buyer, covering ad-account patterns, new ICPs, and experiments.
- Run giveaways and bonuses, such as cash, cameras, mics, and Meta glasses.
- Hold in-person retreats for top creators. Wispr is flying its top 6 to the SF office for a week.

## 5. Scale deliberately
- Grow in steps of 5, then 10, 20, and 40, keeping the culture intact at each step. Wispr went from 5 to 50 active creators in about 4 months.
- At 40–60 creators, coast. From there, only add top-tier people to fill specific gaps. You rarely need more than 60.
- **Referral bonus:** pay an existing creator (Wispr pays $100) for bringing in a peer at or above their own level. Vet each referral's IG or portfolio and take no beginners.
- Keep the bar high. Wispr got about 7,000 marketplace applications and accepted only 5–7.

## 6. Fight creative stagnation
- Burnt-out creators copy the leaderboard's top formats. Meta then clusters those copies under the original and suppresses them.
- Push creators who are in a rut toward genuinely new concepts. The leaderboard leaders win by experimenting, not by copying.
- Use the weekly call to assign new angles, ICPs, and formats.

## Checklist
- [ ] Pick a platform or tracking method that attributes spend or GMV to each creator's ads
- [ ] Set the commission % and the smart retainer (quota and minimum)
- [ ] Audit the ad library for gaps, then recruit 4–5 creators to fill them (100–200 DMs)
- [ ] Set up the Calendly 1:1 link, the weekly call, and a bonus budget
- [ ] Scale 5, 10, 20, 40, then cap around 60, with a referral bonus and vetting
- [ ] Review monthly for near-duplicate creatives and push new formats

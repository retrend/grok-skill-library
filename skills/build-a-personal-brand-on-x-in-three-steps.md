# Goal
Build an X account that earns reach, trust and conversions, using a three-step crash course: craft the brand, set content pillars, execute content.

# Inputs
- [YOUR_SKILLS] and the hot niches you could serve ([NICHE_CANDIDATES])
- [OFFER_OR_PRODUCT_LINK] (optional)
- Time budget per post, which decides the posting strategy in step 3

# Step 1: Craft the brand
1. Find your pocket. Overlap a hot niche (the video's examples: AI, tech, startups, crypto) with your skills. The overlap is your pocket.
2. Pick audience pain points. Ask what your younger self (the video says 16) would want to consume to upskill in this niche.
3. Study the top 10 to 20 accounts in the niche (the video uses an AI assistant to list them). For each, note: formats (articles, threads, videos), messaging (how they package hooks and address pain points), and gaps. Find gaps by reading their comments for critiques and questions, then turn those into your own content.
4. Profile basics:
   - Bio: three lines at most, just say what you do.
   - Profile picture: your real face (the video claims this beats 80% of the competition; unverified).
   - Banner: a plain colour or a simple quote.
   - CTA (optional): a link to a product in the bio, not contact details.

# Step 2: Content pillars
5. Treat X as top of funnel: generate impressions fast and send people to other platforms (the video's example: YouTube).
6. Split content roughly: 60 to 70% top of funnel (trends, viral posts, infographics, news, broad niche topics), about 30% trust and personal (personal takes, videos), about 10% conversion (product or off-platform offer, never pushy).

# Step 3: Execute content
7. Be value-driven: how-tos and full guides, plus "only you can say" posts drawn from your own network and experience. Skip aimless takes.
8. Own a niche: don't copy existing accounts; define a specific category.
9. Show proof: impressions generated, case studies, a video of you talking to a client about their pain points.
10. Pick one posting strategy and don't mix them:
   - Quality: 3 to 5 posts a day, at least 3 hours apart, about 3 hours of work per post.
   - Quantity: up to 24 posts a day, at least 1 hour apart, for news-style or fast niches. Higher flop risk.
11. Package each post: a curiosity-gap hook, a clear CTA that tells people exactly what to do (the video's example: "We work with founders that are struggling to write content. DM me this word..."), and a mix of formats across long and short video and long and short written posts. [HOOK_EXAMPLES: the video gives none; add your own.]

# Avoid
- Negativity: politics and hate on other creators; reports and mutes hurt reach. Block haters instead.
- Reply-guy spam.
- AI slop: low-effort AI-generated replies.

# Success check
- Profile, niche statement and the three-bucket content mix are written down.
- A posting strategy is chosen and followed for a set period ([REVIEW_PERIOD]), then reviewed against impressions and conversions. (added, not in source)

# Not for
- Automated posting or reply bots.
- Paid growth or audience purchases.
- Treating the percentages and frequencies as proven; they are the author's opinion.

# Pod Satire virality playbook

Craft before generation. The live profile holds the job, narrator, credit cap, upload rules and schedule. Comedy Morgue now runs one 16 to 22 minute episode a week plus 5 Shorts cut from it.

## Packaging

Title, thumbnail and first 5 seconds promise the same payoff; rewrite until they match. No misleading titles or thumbnails.

Thumbnails: text at most 4 words, high contrast, one subject. Weekly: stamp or reaction energy with drama in the title. Prestige: title-chain or poster card, no face farm.

Until the channel is phone-verified, custom thumbnails can't be uploaded and YouTube picks a frame. So the title carries the promise, and the opening frame should work as a thumbnail. Still draft thumbnails so they're ready the day verification happens.

## Hooks

Most craft goes into the first spoken line and first frame. AI hook drafts are structure only; the final line is in Comedy Morgue's voice. Patterns: cold-open source clip then thesis (weekly); narrator cold open then thesis (prestige); contrast (claim then ad cut); stakes-first. Remap emotion and structure from a hook bank; never clone wording.

## Formats

One format per video.

1. Weekly autopsy (16 to 22 min): cold-open clip, thesis, clip and voice-over loops, pattern call, payoff. Chapters on.
2. Prestige essay (on request): cold open, thesis, case stack, self-aware beat, soft close.
3. Follow-the-money: juxtapose claim and ad read; source every fact, and keep opinion readable as opinion in the narration (no on-screen satire tag or spoken disclaimer). Sponsor-focused episodes go out as Comedy Ombudsman.
4. Shorts: one idea, 35 to 50s (or 25 to 35s template), information change every 4 to 6s, end on payoff with no outro, distinct first frame, at most one brief muted or ducked reference with a source tag.

## Shorts batch

Cut 5 Shorts from each weekly episode. Each takes a different beat, not the same joke trimmed. Schedule one a day at 12:00 PM on the 5 days after the episode. Never two uploads on the same day.

## Retention

Open a loop in the first 20s and pay it off; cut dead air; pattern interrupt every few beats (clip, sting, card, contradiction); end on payoff, not outro padding. Soft subscribe ask only after a delivered beat.

## Engagement

One specific question about the argument or the cut. Until pinned comments are available, post it as a normal comment once the video is live, as part of the approved list. Invite disagreement. Never fake giveaways, sub4sub, engagement bait, or seeded wrong-take burner comments.

## Shorts versus long-form

Shorts win on first frame, paced changes and completion. Long-form wins on thesis, chapters and clip-to-voice-over rhythm. No daily clip dumps that read as reused content; commentary carries every video.

## Metric mapping

Data sources: views, watch time, average view duration and percentage viewed from the Markifact procedure (7 days after upload); CTR and impressions only from Steven's Studio Advanced-mode CSV. Without the CSV, say packaging can't be judged yet.

Map drops. Viewers leaving in about the first 3s means hook or packaging. A mid-video drop means an unpaid loop. A topic outlier means make more of it. Low CTR with good retention means packaging; good CTR with poor retention means the video broke the promise. Label hypotheses versus measured. Don't flip the whole format after one weak video; wait for at least 4 weekly episodes (placeholder floor Steven can change).

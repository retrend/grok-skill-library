# Pod Satire virality playbook

Craft before generation. The live profile still holds the job, narrator, credit cap and upload rules.

## Packaging

Title, thumbnail and first 5 seconds promise the same payoff; rewrite until they match. Thumb text at most 4 words, high contrast, one subject. Weekly: stamp or reaction energy with drama in the title. Prestige: title-chain or poster card, no face farm. No misleading thumbs or titles.

## Hooks

Most open craft goes into first spoken line and first frame. AI hook drafts are structure only; final line is Comedy Morgue voice. Patterns: cold-open source clip then thesis (weekly); narrator cold open then thesis (prestige); contrast (claim then ad cut); stakes-first. Remap emotion and structure from a hook bank; never clone wording.

## Formats

Use one format per video.

1. Weekly autopsy: cold-open clip, thesis, 3 to 5 clip/VO loops, pattern call, payoff.
2. Prestige essay: cold open, thesis, case stack, self-aware beat, soft close.
3. Follow-the-money: juxtapose claim and ad read; label satire or opinion; source every fact.
4. Shorts: one idea, 35 to 50s (or 25 to 35s template), info change every 4 to 6s, end on payoff with no outro; distinct first frame; at most one brief muted or ducked reference with source tag.

## Retention

Open a loop in the first 20s and pay it off; cut dead air; pattern interrupt every few beats (clip, sting, card, contradiction); end on payoff, not outro padding. Chapters on weekly. Soft subscribe ask only after a delivered beat.

## Engagement

One specific pinned question about the argument or cut. Invite disagreement. Never fake giveaways, sub4sub, engagement bait, or seeded wrong-take burner comments.

## Shorts versus long-form

Shorts win on first frame, paced changes, completion. Long-form wins on thesis, chapters, clip-to-VO rhythm. No daily clip dumps that read as reused content; commentary carries the video.

## Metric mapping

Map drops. Leave in about 3s means hook or packaging. A mid drop means an unpaid loop. A topic outlier means make more. Label hypotheses versus measured. Do not flip the whole format after one weak video.

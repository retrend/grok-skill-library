name: Rewrite AI drafts so they read like a person
description: >-
  Use this when a draft sounds machine-written (even structure, stiff tone, too
  much polish) and you want a fixed set of rewrite passes to make it read like a
  person, without faking experience or blurring facts.
---
The source's hook ("PEOPLE KNOW WHEN YOU'RE USING CHATGPT AND CLAUDE") is the author's claim, not a proven rule. The useful part is the seven prompts.

Run them only on text you believe is true. Don't invent memories, clients, results or hesitations. For financial-adviser or other regulated copy, keep every fact checkable. (added, not in source)

The thread posted the prompts out of order; use the numbered order below. Put the draft in <PASTE_TEXT>.

## Steps
1. **Thought flow correction**
   ```
   This isn't a grammatical correction. It's a reasoning correction. Rewrite it so the ideas move like a real mind: irregular in some places, straight to the point in others, sometimes slower. Break any pattern where the writing feels too controlled. Text: <PASTE_TEXT>
   ```
2. **The pattern breaker**
   ```
   You're an expert at spotting the tells that give away AI-generated text. Go through this piece and wipe them out, one by one. Shake up the structure, ease out any stiff phrasing, and make every word feel like it was picked right then and there, not churned out by some formula. Text: <PASTE_TEXT>
   ```
3. **The credibility test**
   ```
   You're a reader who immediately distrusts texts that seem too polished. Go sentence by sentence and rewrite anything that feels overly constructed, overly corrected, or too precise. It should sound like something a real person said out loud without second-guessing every word. Text: <PASTE_TEXT>
   ```
4. **The voice shaper**
   ```
   You're building a voice that seems to belong to a real person. Rewrite it to carry a clear point of view, not the tone of a neutral observer trying to please everyone. Let opinions, small contradictions, and natural shifts in tone appear. Text: <PASTE_TEXT>
   ```
   Keep only honest contradictions (a trade-off you actually accept). No fake personality. (added, not in source)
5. **The imperfections injector**
   ```
   Perfect texts have no history. Rewrite by adding micro-imperfections that only someone who has lived the subject would write: a strong opinion here, a hesitation there, an incomplete sentence where it makes sense. Do not correct afterward. Text: <PASTE_TEXT>
   ```
   A strong opinion is fine if it's yours. A made-up hesitation or "I was there" detail is not. (added, not in source)
6. **The audio test**
   ```
   Read this text out loud in your head. Mark each sentence where you would make a strange pause or where the rhythm would break in a real conversation. Rewrite those sentences so that the entire text flows like human speech, not like a document. Text: <PASTE_TEXT>
   ```
   Then actually read the result out loud. (added, not in source)
7. **The soul detector**
   ```
   Read this text as if you've already seen thousands of AI-generated texts. Identify the 3 sentences that sound the most artificial and rewrite each one as if the author were talking to a close friend. No filter. No perfection. Text: <PASTE_TEXT>
   ```
   Finish with a factual pass: names, figures, offers and advice still accurate. (added, not in source)

## Success check
- Sentence shapes vary and there's a point of view you'll stand behind.
- It reads aloud without tripping on padded phrasing.
- No new facts, clients, memories or results appeared.
- Regulated or offer copy still says only what you can support.

## Anti-patterns
- Overdoing it into a new obvious style (forced fragments, random slang, fake typos).
- Adding lived experience you didn't live.
- Hiding AI authorship where a platform, client or regulator requires disclosure. (added, not in source)
- Treating "people can always tell" as a research finding.
